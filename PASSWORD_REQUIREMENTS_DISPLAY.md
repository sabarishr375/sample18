## To See the Changes:

1. **Refresh your browser** at http://localhost:5173
2. The page should auto-reload (Vite hot reload)
3. If not, press **Ctrl+F5** (Windows) or **Cmd+Shift+R** (Mac)
4. Navigate to **Login** or **Register** page
5. You'll see the password requirements displayed below the password field!
